Toonfork is a calm, offline reader for vertical scrolling comics. Download it for free and start right away: 15 stories are already inside the app. No account, no sign-up.

MADE FOR YOUR THUMB
Every story is built for the phone: one long strip that you scroll from top to bottom. Selected panels come alive as short animated loops – rain on a window, light on the water, wind in someone's hair.

FREE GAMEBOOKS AND DRAMAS
• Sara and the Black Crown – an adventure for kids where you choose the way. Two endings to find.
• Learning to Be Silent – a chamber drama set in a therapist's office. You sit in the psychologist's chair and decide what to say.
• Metal Sky Above Us – a father and son in a megacity under acid rain, followed through five acts from childhood to middle age.
• The Blooming Meadow That Turned Grey – twenty years of one family, painted in watercolour that fades to grey scene by scene.
The dramas are written for adults. They deal with an eating disorder, thoughts of death and difficult family relationships.

STORY PACKS
Want more? Optional packs add up to 50 stories each:
• Romance – light love stories for adult readers, nothing explicit. From Paris to Kyoto.
• Kids – gentle stories for younger readers.
• Superheroes – action and big adventures with a comic-book wink.
One bundle, "Everything", unlocks every pack, including packs released later. Downloaded packs stay readable offline.

12 LANGUAGES
Every story and gamebook can be read in 12 languages: Czech, English, German, French, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Russian and Ukrainian. Switch the language of the speech bubbles at any time.

FOR KIDS AND GROWN-UPS
On first launch you pick a profile. The Kids profile hides romance and the adult dramas. Every purchase and every switch to the Adults profile is protected by a parental gate.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Toonfork collects no personal data. Purchases are handled by Google Play, and what you own is remembered on your device.
