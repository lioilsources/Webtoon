
## Chamber drama (overrides everything above about romance and fashion)
This is a quiet two-hander between a psychologist and his patient. There is NO romance: they never touch, never kiss, never lean together, no blush, no couple framing. Clothes are plain and never change. Ignore "Fashion is the point".
- TEXT IS SACRED. Every line of dialogue in the screenplay appears in a bubble, word for word, in the given order. Never paraphrase, shorten, merge, reorder or add a line. Split a long line over two bubbles at a sentence end if needed. No narration except what the screenplay gives as narration.
- Lines of one exchange may share a panel (max 2 bubbles). Silent panels are welcome when the screenplay says silence, a pause or the clock: empty armchair, glass of water on the table, the wall clock, the window, the painting.
- Two characters in a panel: `wide` or `over-shoulder`, sitting apart in two armchairs, a low table between them.
- The body is never a subject of the picture. Anna is always in her long sweater. Faces, hands holding sleeves, the glass, the window, the painting. Never draw food, a scale, a mirror, a measuring tape, bare arms, waist, ribs, or thinness; never use the tags thin, skinny, slim, underweight, gaunt. `base` uses `slender` at most.
- Use only the locations the screenplay names, plus "Poradna – okno" and "Poradna – obraz" for panels of the window and the painting.
- `animate`: at most 1 panel, ambient only (rain on the window, curtain, light moving).
