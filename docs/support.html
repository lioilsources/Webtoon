Frequently Asked Questions

I bought a pack on another device or reinstalled the app. How do I get it back?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. On the App Store, Family Sharing members get packs too.

A pack or gamebook does not download.

Downloads need an internet connection and enough free space (a pack is about 50–150 MB). Try again on Wi-Fi; an interrupted download resumes. Once installed, everything works offline.

How do I change the reading language?

Settings → reading language. Every story and gamebook can be read in 12 languages: Czech, English, German, Italian, French, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Russian and Ukrainian.

What is the Kids profile?

The Kids profile hides romance stories, packs and the adult dramas. Purchases and switching to the Adults profile are behind a parental gate.

How do gamebooks work?

At the end of a chapter you pick what happens next. Your progress and the endings you found are saved on the device; you can step back or start again at any time.

Some stories deal with difficult topics.

The adult dramas include an eating disorder and thoughts of death. If any of it concerns you, please reach out: in Europe call 116 123, or your local crisis line.
